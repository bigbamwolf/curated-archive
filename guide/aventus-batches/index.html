Creed Aventus, 35 batches compared

Every note below was written from the bottle itself, in Manila

Aventus is not one fragrance. Creed leans on natural materials, naturals move with the harvest, and the formula has been revised across the years. Two bottles with the same name and different codes on the base genuinely smell different. Most of what is written about this online is copied between forums by people who have smelled one or two batches. What follows is 35 bottles that live on one shelf, described honestly, including the ones that are hard to like.

Nobody publishes an official key to the codes, so if you want to know how to read the code itself, that is here. This page is about how they smell.

Smoky

Aventus 10J02 also sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 10J02 is all about its smoke, which comes through with an ashtray like character that makes the scent feel very bold and challenging. It is not the easiest batch to appreciate right away, since the smokiness has an acquired taste and leans more rugged than crowd pleasing. In the mid, the fragrance stays woody and smoky, then gradually softens into a creamy musk vanilla drydown that gives the batch a smoother and more comforting finish.

Aventus 11Z01 sold out also pineapple forward, fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy
Aventus 11Z01 has no noticeable pineapple presence and instead leans heavily into a smoky, oceanic, and salty profile that feels very distinctive from the usual fruity Aventus expectation. In the mid, a strong, clean, crisp, and almost pungent musk takes over, giving the fragrance a sharp and powerful character. As it dries down, the smokiness returns and leaves a lasting impression, but the smoke here feels especially unique, unusual, and difficult to compare to anything else, which is what makes this batch stand out so much.

Aventus 12D01 also pineapple forward, fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy
Aventus 12D01 opens with a distinct grilled pineapple note, as if the fruit has light charred marks, giving it a bold and memorable introduction. In the mid, the profile turns citrusy and earthy, adding depth and structure without losing freshness. The drydown reveals a smooth citrusy vanilla that feels refined and well integrated. The transitions between stages are seamless and satisfying, fully living up to the batch’s reputation, with the scent remaining clearly detectable even after 8 hours.

Aventus 12F01 also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 12F01 is dominated by an intense, in your face smoke that immediately gives the fragrance a raw, almost burned woodland character. The smokiness is bold and uncompromising, yet it remains surprisingly wearable, showing the kind of refined and distinctive smoke that makes the original Aventus difficult to replicate. In the mid, a hint of fruitiness tries to come through, but the smoke stays firmly in control for nearly three hours before the scent finally softens into a musky vanilla drydown. It is a powerful and memorable batch that leans heavily into smoke while still ending on a smoother, more comforting finish.

Aventus 13ZZ01 also pineapple forward, fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy
Aventus 13ZZ01 opens with a strong burst of fruity pineapple, paired with a pungent musk and a well judged touch of smoke that gives the scent both brightness and depth. In the mid, a subtle saltiness emerges alongside the smokiness, adding a slightly rugged and more textured character to the composition. As it dries down, the fragrance becomes more citrusy and musky, leaving a cleaner but still distinctive finish. Performance is solid, with around 2 hours of projection and about 5 hours on skin.

Aventus 14F01 also pineapple forward, fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy
Aventus 14F01 opens with a fruity and musky profile that feels fresh and immediately wearable. In the mid, a faint whisper of smoke appears but remains subtle and barely noticeable. The fragrance maintains a fresh pineapple accord through to the drydown, giving it a clean, bright, and consistent finish.

Aventus 14K01 also pineapple forward, fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 14K01 is a stark departure from the modern fruity interpretations of Aventus, opening with a deep blend of earthy, mossy, and smoky notes that immediately signal a mature and authoritative character. The depth is striking, evoking a true time capsule effect that feels old-world and seasoned rather than playful. Despite the absence of a fruity opening, the Aventus DNA remains unmistakable, even recognizable to others. In the mid, a subtle pineapple note begins to surface, sharpened by assertive woods that add structure and edge. The drydown introduces a mix of saltiness, musk, and vanilla, grounding the fragrance into a powerful and long-lasting base. Performance is exceptional, with around 7 hours of projection and up to 20 hours of longevity.

Aventus 16E01 sold out also pineapple forward, fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 16E01 – A sparkling pineapple effect almost fizzy in brightness, grounded by touches of ash-smoke for depth. The heart develops an airy woodiness with a salinic edge: a rare and sophisticated nuance within the Aventus family. Longevity is solid, with extended bright projection before settling into a fresh citrus-musky finale.

Aventus 17W01 sold out also fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 17W01, A bergamot forward entry with crisp brightness, maturing swiftly into a subtly smoky masculinity. The drydown glows with creamy vanilla and wood, creating an alluring signature trail that remains perceptible even through a face mask. Assertive yet polished.

Aventus F388 also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus F388 - Green-woody and ambroxan-forward with understated citrus presence. The heart releases a micro-hint of smoke before landing in a vanilla-musk Aventus base. A young-feeling batch destined to grow richer with maturity and time.

Aventus F463 sold out also pineapple forward, fruity, sweet and creamy
Aventus Flacon F463, A refined balance of bright pineapple and birch smoke up front, followed by a dignified smoky heart and a warm sweet musky closing. Classic, confident, and true to the quintessential Aventus spirit.

Pineapple forward

Aventus 15R11 also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 15R11 – A radiant expression of pineapple brightness, opening with sweet vibrancy before evolving into a refined woody signature. The iconic sweetness gently shifts into a softly zesty brightness reminiscent of fine lemon sorbet, wrapped in musk and dry woods. A confident, uplifting, and easy-wearing Aventus that brings optimism with subtle masculine structure.

Aventus 15Y01 also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 15Y01 - Bright, joyful pineapple leads the composition from the very first spray, balanced by a touch of moss and airy woodiness. The fruit remains expressive before gracefully giving way to the beloved musk-vanilla warmth. A vibrant and cheerful batch that performs exceptionally on clothing, retaining its juicy radiance throughout the day.

Aventus 15Y11 also fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 15Y11 – Opening with a lightly earthy fruitiness, this batch immediately leans dry-woody and aromatic. Lemon and woods define the heart, giving it a clean and distinguished identity. As it matures on skin, a gentle pineapple-musk veil emerges with remarkable persistence even activating beautifully with post-shower steam. A quiet powerhouse with impressive staying power.

Aventus 16A01 also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 16A01 - A bright, lightly sweet pineapple character reminiscent of airy confectionery fruit. The woodiness in the heart remains subdued, giving this batch a breezy, elegant manner. The drydown is purely musky, maintaining a gentle, understated charm that never overwhelms.

Aventus 16D01 also fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 16D01 - A fresh pineapple opening with balanced sweetness over subtle effervescence. The journey continues with clean woods weaving into the fruit, forming a respectful harmony. The drydown is citrus-musky, calm yet confident — a fragrance with unspoken authority rather than loud projection.

Aventus 16K01 also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 16K01 - A darker, more mature pineapple begins the experience, infused with a soft smokiness and earthy undertones. The mid reveals a clarified pineapple note with restrained woods, evolving into a warm musk sweetness. Subtle, quietly sensual, and remarkably smooth in transition.

Aventus 17T01 also fruity, sweet and creamy
A refined and beautifully balanced interpretation of the Aventus DNA, 17T01 opens with a green-toned pineapple that feels fresh, natural, and effortlessly classy. As it settles, the fruit evolves into a smoother, richer pineapple accord with gentle sweetness, evoking depth without becoming candied. The drydown reveals a graceful fusion of vanilla and soft musk, creating an aura of subtly seductive sophistication. Long-lasting and elegantly composed, 17T01 delivers presence without aggression — a confident, modern classic with timeless appeal.

Aventus 17X01 also fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy, woody
A refined and luminous interpretation of the Aventus style. The opening presents a crisp and uplifting pineapple brightness intertwined with a gentle wood undertone that feels clean and polished. As it transitions, the fragrance deepens with a sweeter pineapple nuance lightly accented by a delicate spark of bergamot for subtle energy and lift. The drydown reveals a soft veil of musk and quiet warmth, creating an elegant skin-close aura. Fresh, balanced, and effortlessly sophisticated — a scent that communicates clarity and class rather than volume or aggression.

Aventus 18D02 also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 18D01 opens with an overripe pineapple accord paired with light woods, giving it a rich but controlled introduction. As it moves into the mid, the profile becomes fruity and musky, maintaining a soft and understated character. In the drydown, a very subtle musky vanilla emerges on both skin and fabric, requiring close wear to fully appreciate. Even after 20 hours, the scent remains detectable on clothing, with occasional gentle wafts of projection.

Aventus 18F21 also fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 18F21 - A crisp pineapple impression that stays fresh rather than sugary, gradually acquiring woody grounding as it develops. The base is bright, citrus-musky, and uplifting. A modern-feeling batch that enhances vitality and clean energy.

Aventus 18K01 also fruity, fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 18K01 - A lively burst of sweet pineapple transitions into a refined woody-citrus midtone with bergamot presence. Linear in progression, but assured in performance, especially on clothing. A dependable, clean-cut batch ideal for both skin and fabric application.

Aventus 21V01 sold out also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 21V01 - Vibrant pineapple meets earthy patchouli in a striking contrast, followed by a structured woody phase and a citrus-musky finish. Extremely persistent on fabric. Lasting well beyond a full day, and maintaining presence with understated sophistication.

Aventus F2838 sold out also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus F2838, A playful pineapple atop a soft woody dryness that becomes pepper tinged and lively through the heart. The drydown divides: citrus musky on skin, boldly woody on fabric. A striking character with evolving duality and excellent reactivation after heat exposure.

Aventus F3135 sold out also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus F3135 - Blackcurrant and patchouli dominate the opening with bold personality before pineapple rises through the composition. The finale is an intensely woody signature softened by intermittent musk hints. For connoisseurs who appreciate unapologetically masculine woods.

Aventus F3334 sold out also fruity, sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus F3334 - An exquisite burst of sweet, syrupy pineapple opens the scent before shifting quickly to woody earthiness. The mid is gracefully woody-fresh, and the drydown becomes citrus-musky with outstanding longevity that clings to clothing. A strikingly dynamic batch with satisfying evolution.

Fruity

Aventus 15X21 also sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 15X21 - An unapologetically woody interpretation of the Aventus DNA. The fruitiness remains understated, surrendering quickly to deep, assertive woods that command presence. As it settles, the fragrance mellows into a smooth musk-vanilla warmth, leaving behind a gentlemanly aura that resonates quietly but firmly.

Aventus 16H01 also fresh and aquatic, woody
Aventus 16H01 opens with a refined tart fruitiness that feels bright and composed rather than aggressive. The mid transitions into a fresh, citrus-forward profile that remains clean and airy, giving the fragrance an easy sense of movement and clarity. As it dries down, subtle woods emerge to provide balance and structure, preventing the composition from becoming too sharp or fleeting. Overall, this batch leans toward the lighter side of the Aventus spectrum. It is smooth, approachable, and effortless to wear, making it a dependable option for daily use and warmer conditions without sacrificing the signature Aventus character.

Aventus 17Q11 also woody
Aventus 17Q11 - A perfectly calibrated harmony of wooded depth and juicy fruit, silky and beautifully structured. Devoid of harsh smokiness, this batch speaks to collectors who appreciate purity and balance within the Aventus profile. Smooth, elegant, and exceptionally refined.

Aventus 17S01 also sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 17S01 opens with a woody and earthy profile that immediately feels grounded and mature. As it moves into the mid, the woodiness becomes warmer and ambery, supported by a subtle touch of vanilla that adds smoothness. In the drydown, the scent shifts toward a citrusy, musky finish that lightens the overall composition. Longevity is strong, lasting around 11 hours on both skin and T shirt.

Aventus 20J01 also sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 20J01 presents a darker character, comparable in depth to 16M01, but with its own distinct personality. The opening is earthy and woody, immediately setting a more serious and grounded tone, with a noticeable inky nuance reminiscent of a pentel pen that adds an interesting, almost industrial edge. As it develops into the mid, the woody profile remains dominant, but a subtle vanilla nuance begins to surface, softening the darkness and adding quiet richness. In the drydown, citrus and musk emerge to lift the composition, creating contrast against the earlier heaviness while maintaining the recognizable Aventus DNA. Notably, the scent shows strong persistence, with traces of the DNA lingering on the skin even after washing with soap, highlighting its tenacity and staying power.

Aventus 21T01 sold out also sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus 21T01 presents a darker character, comparable in depth to 16M01, but with its own distinct personality. The opening is earthy and woody, immediately setting a more serious and grounded tone, with a noticeable inky nuance reminiscent of a pentel pen that adds an interesting, almost industrial edge. As it develops into the mid, the woody profile remains dominant, but a subtle vanilla nuance begins to surface, softening the darkness and adding quiet richness. In the drydown, citrus and musk emerge to lift the composition, creating contrast against the earlier heaviness while maintaining the recognizable Aventus DNA. Notably, the scent shows strong persistence, with traces of the DNA lingering on the skin even after washing with soap, highlighting its tenacity and staying power.

Aventus 21X01B also fresh and aquatic, sweet and creamy
Aventus 21X01B opens with a fresh, musky bergamot that feels clean and airy. The mid stays citrusy and musky, maintaining the same light and transparent character. In the drydown, it simplifies further into a soft musky base. Overall, it comes across as extremely light, likely due to minimal oxidation at this stage.

Aventus F1520 sold out also sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus F1520 - A distinctive opening of deep woods and patchouli that later reveals bright blackcurrant flexibility. The closing impression is bergamot-kissed musk, creating a transition from shadow to light. A fascinating introspective batch that evolves with age, worthy of cellaring for maturity.

Aventus F387 sold out also sweet and creamy, woody
Aventus F387 - Bergamot-leaning with gentle vanilla and woods maintaining equilibrium. Vanilla shades emerge more on skin, woods more on fabric, resulting in a dual-character drydown. Promising in longevity and likely to improve beautifully through oxidation over time.
