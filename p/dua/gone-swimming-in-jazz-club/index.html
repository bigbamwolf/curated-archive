Dua Gone Swimming in Jazz Club

Dua archive

Gone Swimming in Jazz Club opens with a striking contrast of fizzy orange brightness from Afternoon Swim layered against the smoky depth of Jazz Club. The combination is bold and immediately attention grabbing, though it can verge on being dizzying, making it better suited for cold environments. As the fragrance develops, the composition quickly leans into the familiar character of Jazz Club, which remains dominant through the mid and into the drydown, resulting in a warm, smoky, and consistent finish.
