Fragrance World Black Leather

Middle Eastern archive

Aventus inspired. Black Leather is a fragrance that leans into a very fruity pineapple profile balanced by just the right amount of woodiness. The pineapple comes through bright, juicy, and appealing, while the woody base adds enough structure to keep the scent grounded and masculine. The result is a smooth and well balanced composition that feels easy to enjoy, especially for those who prefer a fruit forward take with a refined woody backbone.
