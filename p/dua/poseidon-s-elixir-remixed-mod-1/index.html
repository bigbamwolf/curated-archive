Dua Poseidon's Elixir Remixed MOD 1

Dua archive

Poseidon’s Elixir MOD 1, a combination of 11Z01 x 13ZZ01, opens with a strong salty, musky, and aquatic character that immediately dominates the top. As it moves into the mid, the intensity becomes more subdued but remains largely unchanged, with the fruity pineapple character of an Aventus batch being almost unrecognizable. In the drydown, the fruitiness of 13ZZ01 finally emerges, though it is still laced with lingering saltiness. Overall, the scent comes across as aggressive and overwhelming, to the point of being dizzying and unsettling.
