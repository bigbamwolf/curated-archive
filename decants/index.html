What is a perfume decant?

A decant is a smaller amount of a fragrance transferred from the original full bottle into a sealed atomiser. It is the same juice from the same bottle, in a size you can actually finish, at a price you can actually test.

How much does a perfume decant cost in the Philippines?

At Curated Archive a 1ml decant starts at PHP 21, and the price scales with the size and the house. A niche fragrance that costs several thousand pesos for a full bottle can be tried for the price of a coffee.

What sizes do decants come in?

1ml, 2ml, 5ml and 10ml. 1ml and 2ml are for testing a scent properly across a full day. 5ml and 10ml are for a fragrance you already know you want to wear.

Are decants authentic?

Every decant here is poured from a full bottle that is photographed and kept on the shelf. For Creed Aventus the batch code of the exact bottle is published on the product page, which is something a sealed box cannot tell you.
