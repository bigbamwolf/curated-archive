All Sales Are Final

Every Curated Archive order is a fragrance decant, poured to order from an
authenticated source bottle at the time you check out. Because each
decant is opened, measured, and sealed specifically for your order, we
are unable to accept returns, refunds, or exchanges once an order has
shipped.

This applies regardless of the reason, including:
- Personal scent preference or change of mind
- How the fragrance performs on your own skin chemistry
- Projection, longevity, or sillage not matching expectations

We encourage you to research a batch or scent (reviews, notes, our own
descriptions) before ordering, since decants cannot be resold once opened.

Damage or Breakage in Transit

Every decant is packed securely to survive standard courier handling. If
your order arrives broken, leaking, or damaged in transit:
1. Do not discard the packaging or the item.
2. Take a video of your unboxing if possible — this is your evidence.
3. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos or video of the
   damage and the shipping label.
4. Breakage claims are filed with the courier (J&T Express, LBC Express,
   Grab Express, or Lalamove, depending on which you selected at
   checkout), since the courier is responsible for the item once it
   leaves our hands in good condition. We will help you file and support
   the claim, but the courier settles it.

Wrong Item or Missing Item

If you receive the wrong fragrance, wrong size, or an item is missing
from your order, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of
what you received plus your order confirmation. We will make it right at
no cost to you, this is the one exception to the all-sales-final rule
since it is our error, not a matter of preference.

Custom Hybrid / Frankenbatch Orders

Any custom blended order (Hybrid or Frankenbatch) is final on order and
carries no refund, remake, or revision under any circumstance once
blending has started, since the result is created specifically to your
specification and cannot be undone or resold. You confirm this by
checking the agreement box before submitting a hybrid order.

Cancellations

Orders can only be cancelled before they are decanted and packed. Once
you receive a payment confirmation from us, the order is queued for
decanting and can no longer be cancelled or changed.

How to Reach Us

For any breakage claim, wrong item, or missing item, message us on the
same channel where you ordered (TikTok Shop chat, Shopee chat, Instagram
DM, or the website concierge) with your order number.
