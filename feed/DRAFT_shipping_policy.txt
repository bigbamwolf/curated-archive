Where We Ship

Curated Archive ships within the Philippines only. We do not currently
offer international shipping. All orders are dispatched from our base in
Princeville, Mandaluyong City, Metro Manila.

Processing Time

Orders are decanted, sealed, and dispatched within 1 to 3 working days
after your payment is confirmed. Payment is confirmed once your Xendit
checkout clears, or once your GCash payment is verified for orders that
use the GCash fallback method.

Courier Options and Rates

At checkout, choose the courier that fits your timeline:

  J&T Express      approx PHP 95    2 to 5 working days
  LBC Express      approx PHP 160   1 to 3 working days
  Grab Express     approx PHP 260   Same day, Metro Manila only
  Lalamove         approx PHP 360   Same day, Metro Manila only

Courier fees are calculated at checkout and added to your total.

Free Shipping

Orders totaling PHP 10,000 or more ship free, regardless of courier
chosen. Below that threshold, the courier fee you select is added to
your order total.

Delivery Estimates

Estimated delivery windows above start counting from the day your order
is dispatched, not the day it is placed. Same-day couriers (Grab
Express, Lalamove) are only available for Metro Manila addresses and
same-day delivery depends on order cutoff time and courier availability
that day.

Insurance and Packaging

Every decant is insured and packed to withstand standard courier
handling: sealed, cushioned, and boxed to prevent leaks or breakage in
transit. See our Returns & Refund Policy for what to do if an order
arrives damaged.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, we will provide your tracking number or waybill
through the same channel you ordered on (TikTok Shop, Shopee, Instagram
DM, or the website concierge).

Address Accuracy

Please make sure your shipping address, contact number, and landmark
details are complete and accurate at checkout. We are not responsible
for delays or non-delivery caused by an incomplete or incorrect address
provided at the time of order.
