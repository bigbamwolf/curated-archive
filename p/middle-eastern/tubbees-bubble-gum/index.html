Tubbees Bubble Gum

Middle Eastern archive

Bubble Gum opens with a bright, minty sweetness that can initially come across like a child's toothpaste, giving it a playful and slightly nostalgic freshness. After a closer smell, the scent shifts into something much more familiar, evoking the distinctly sweet, chewy character of classic Bazooka bubble gum. The result is a fragrance that feels fun, youthful, and candy like, with a sugary profile that leans heavily into childhood nostalgia.
